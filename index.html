Chapter 2

How I think about system design

Scale out

Stateless services on Kubernetes, with Redis caching, so traffic spikes are boring.

Fail gracefully

Retries with exponential backoff, timeouts and fallbacks. Assume every network call can fail.

Events over calls

Kafka, CDC and CQRS keep services loosely coupled and data flowing.

Ground the AI

Good retrieval first: embeddings plus hybrid search, so answers come from your data.

Every design is a trade-off: consistency vs availability, speed vs cost. I like making those choices out loud.
